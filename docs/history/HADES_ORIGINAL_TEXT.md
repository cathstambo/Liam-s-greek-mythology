# Page 1 — Hades

**Title:** HADES  
**Subtitle:** God of the Underworld  
**Pronunciation:** HAY-deez

## Who is Hades?

Deep beneath the earth lies the mysterious Underworld, a kingdom ruled by Hades, one of the most powerful gods in Greek mythology.

Hades is the brother of Zeus and Poseidon. After the three brothers defeated the Titans, they divided the world between them. Zeus took the sky, Poseidon took the sea, and Hades became ruler of the Underworld.

Despite his dark and frightening kingdom, Hades wasn't evil. His job was to keep order among the dead and make sure no souls escaped!

## Quick Facts

### Powers
Controls the riches beneath the earth and can become invisible.

### Symbol
Helm of Darkness and two-pronged sceptre.

### Home
His palace deep in the Underworld.

### Guardian Creature
Cerberus, the three-headed guard dog.

# Page 2 — Hades

## Special Powers

- Wears a magical helmet that makes him invisible.
- Rules over the souls of the dead.
- Controls precious metals and treasures hidden underground.

## Family

- **Brothers:** Zeus and Poseidon
- **Wife:** Persephone, Queen of the Underworld
- **Parents:** Cronus and Rhea

## Did You Know?

Hades had a giant three-headed dog named Cerberus guarding the entrance to the Underworld. His job wasn't to stop people getting in — it was to stop the dead getting out!
